Story

Perfect Crown ek fictional 21st-century constitutional monarchy Korea mein set hai. Seong Hui-ju ek wealthy chaebol family ki heiress hai. Uske paas paisa, beauty aur intelligence sab kuch hai, lekin royal status nahi hai. Dusri taraf Grand Prince Yi An royal family se belong karta hai. Uske paas royal title hai, lekin personal freedom aur real power bahut limited hai. Hui-ju royal status hasil karne ke liye Yi An ke saath ek contract marriage ka proposal rakhti hai. Shuru mein unki shaadi sirf ek agreement hoti hai, lekin jaise-jaise dono ek doosre ko samajhne lagte hain, unke beech feelings develop hone lagti hain. Unhe royal family, political conflicts aur social-status barriers ka saamna karna padta hai. Kya unki contract marriage sachche pyaar mein badal jayegi?
