Filing For Love

Country: South Korea 🇰🇷

Total Episodes: 12

Watch Time: Around 1 hour per episode

Genre: Romantic Comedy

Story

Noh Ki-Jun is an ace member of the audit department at Haemu Group who is on track for a major promotion. However, his life changes when Joo In-Ah becomes the new head of the audit department and demotes him to Audit Team 3, where he has to deal with workplace misconduct and various internal scandals.

At first, Ki-Jun sees In-Ah as his enemy and wants to find a way to get his old position back. While investigating a mysterious allegation involving In-Ah, he begins to discover that she is different from what he initially believed.

As they continue working together, their rivalry slowly turns into an unexpected attraction, bringing romance, comedy and workplace chaos into their lives.
